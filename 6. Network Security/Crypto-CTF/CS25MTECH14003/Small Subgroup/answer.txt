TThe Diffie-Hellman exchange was vulnerable to a small subgroup attack.
By sending B = 1, the shared secret becomes 1 irrespective of the private key.
The AES key was derived from SHA256(shared_secret), which is predictable.
Using shared = 1, I recomputed the AES key and decrypted the ciphertext.
A solve.py script was used to perform key derivation and AES decryption.
